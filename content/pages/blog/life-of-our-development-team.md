A stable weekly workflow improved team reliability more than adding new tooling.

## Monday: Scope and Acceptance Criteria

Each issue had one owner, one deadline, and testable acceptance criteria. Ambiguous tasks were split before development started.

## Tuesday to Thursday: Build and Document

Developers shared short progress notes with assumptions, blockers, and implementation decisions. This reduced hidden drift and made reviews easier.

## Friday: Review and Regression Check

Pull requests were reviewed against accessibility, responsiveness, and basic performance checks. Releases were only approved after checklist completion.

## Outcome

- Fewer last-minute blockers.
- Faster peer review turnaround.
- Better documentation quality for future case studies.
