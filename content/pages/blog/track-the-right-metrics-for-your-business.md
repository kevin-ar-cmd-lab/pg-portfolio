A portfolio update was treated like a product iteration with measurable checkpoints.

## Primary Metrics

- Case study completeness: problem, constraints, decisions, outcomes, lessons.
- Link credibility: visible GitHub, LinkedIn, and resume access points.
- Accessibility hygiene: heading order, form labels, keyboard focus visibility.
- SEO quality: page-level meta title and description relevance.
- Performance behavior: non-critical image loading deferred by default.

## Why These Metrics

Each metric reflected recruiter or reviewer friction directly. The goal was not perfect scores in isolation, but clearer evidence of technical readiness.
