1. Generic template pages reduce portfolio trust.
2. Project pages should explain decisions, not just features.
3. Outcomes must be measurable whenever possible.
4. Content hierarchy is a conversion factor.
5. Clear calls to action reduce recruiter friction.
6. Accessibility signals engineering maturity.
7. External profile links should be easy to find.
8. Resume availability should never be hidden.
9. Performance gains often come from simple defaults.
10. Consistent page structure improves reviewer speed.

Each lesson translated into direct edits across homepage, case studies, and supporting pages, making the portfolio easier to evaluate in a short review window.
