Project pages were rewritten around one goal: let a reviewer understand value quickly without losing technical depth.

## Structure Used

1. **Project Summary** for context in two or three lines.
2. **Problem** to show why the work mattered.
3. **Constraints** to communicate realistic limitations.
4. **Technical Decisions** to demonstrate engineering reasoning.
5. **Accessibility and Performance** to show quality standards.
6. **Outcome** with measurable impact.
7. **What Was Learned** to show growth.

## Why It Worked

- Reviewers could skim consistently across multiple projects.
- Technical detail stayed focused on decisions, not feature lists.
- Measurable outcomes made claims easier to trust.

## Implementation Note

Using the same section headings in every case study reduced writing friction and kept tone consistent across the portfolio.
